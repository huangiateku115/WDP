## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-trust, conversion-optimized commercial service experience tailored for modern urban self-storage facilities. Designed with inspiration from top-tier Southeast Asian logistics and storage providers, the aesthetic emphasizes reliability, cleanliness, operational security, and immediate clarity. 

The emotional tone balances institutional dependability (deep slate navy and crisp royal blue) with energetic retail urgency (vibrant signal orange). The system avoids dark-mode SaaS abstractions or muted software palettes; instead, it relies on crisp white fields, cool atmospheric tints, generous container paddings, physical storage facility imagery, and tactile callouts that build customer confidence for booking, sizing, and reserving physical units online.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate commercial hierarchy:
- **Primary (`#0588CF`)**: Vivid Royal / Sky Blue anchors trust, cleanliness, and brand presence. Applied to brand headers, primary badges, navigation accents, and key promotional panels.
- **Secondary (`#FF9418` / `#FF9818`)**: High-contrast, high-conversion Signal Orange. Reserved strictly for conversion hotspots—primary booking CTAs, promotional discount tags, directional unit selection prompts, and the featured bottom sticky segment.
- **Tertiary & Text Headings (`#12304A` / `#102A43`)**: Deep Industrial Navy. Used for all primary headlines and structural containers, providing maximum contrast against white and light blue surfaces.
- **Body & Neutral (`#2D4B68`)**: Slate Dark. Delivers balanced readability for operational copy, pricing tables, dimensions, and specifications without the harshness of pure black.
- **Backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`, `#F7F9FB`, `#EEF4F9`)**: Crisp white alternating with subtle cool-tinted grays to segment storage unit tiers, customer testimonials, and facility features.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes aggressive legibility, punchy commercial weight, and rapid scannability:
- **Font Family**: Plus Jakarta Sans provides contemporary geometric structure paired with rounded friendliness, preventing the industrial subject matter from feeling sterile.
- **Section Headers**: Standardized section framing pairs a vivid solid orange square glyph (`■`) with an uppercase Royal Blue eyebrow (`eyebrow-bold`), followed immediately by an Extrabold Deep Navy headline (`headline-xl` / `headline-xl-mobile`).
- **Hierarchy Rhythm**: Keep lead paragraphs compact (`body-lg`), and ensure unit sizing parameters (m², m³, dimensions) use tight tabular styling with medium and bold sub-labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum width constraints:
- **Max Canvas Width**: 1200px centered layout for high-density commercial clarity without edge stretching.
- **Section Alternation**: Alternates between clean `#FFFFFF` full-width bands and cool `#F7F9FB` or `#EEF4F9` bands with `section-gap` vertical padding, maintaining structural pacing across multi-unit showcases, size estimators, and price calculators.
- **Mobile Bottom Offset**: To accommodate the permanent multi-action sticky bar on viewports under 768px, page content must include a minimum bottom padding of `5.5rem` (`88px`).

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a clean, high-conversion commercial service aesthetic, depth avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of crisp structural definition:
- **Card Surfaces**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards use subtle borders (`1px solid #D5E2EC`) paired with a light, warm-cool ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.06)`.
- **Active & Hover States**: On user interaction or selection, the card elevates with `0 10px 24px rgba(5, 136, 207, 0.14)` and a primary blue border highlight (`1.5px solid #0588CF`).
- **Persistent Bottom Sticky Bar**: Elevated with an upward commercial shadow: `0 -4px 20px rgba(18, 48, 74, 0.12)` to clearly separate it from scrollable content.
- **Overlays & Modals**: Grounded using a dark translucent backdrop (`rgba(16, 42, 67, 0.6)`) with a clean white container.

## Shapes

The roundedness level is **2** (0.5rem / 8px baseline):
- **Buttons**: Stand at 0.5rem (8px) for secondary and utility elements, while primary conversion buttons utilize 0.75rem (12px) to communicate tactile pressability.
- **Cards & Pricing Columns**: 0.75rem (12px) to 1rem (16px) with clean inner clipping for facility preview images.
- **Status Pills & Eyebrow Badges**: 9999px (full pill) to sharply differentiate micro-labels from actionable rectangular buttons.
- **Eyebrow Accent Icon**: Geometric square `■` (rounded to 2px) flanking section titles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion CTA**: Solid Vibrant Orange (`#FF9818`), white bold text, minimum 48px height (54px in hero sections), rounded-md (8px or 12px), paired with a trailing white directional arrow (`→`). Hover state shifts to `#FF9418` with slight elevation.
- **Secondary Action**: White surface, 2px border in Royal Blue (`#0588CF`), Royal Blue text, smooth transition on hover to light blue fill (`#EEF4F9`).
- **Surface Contrast CTA (on Royal Blue backgrounds)**: Solid Signal Orange button or crisp white button with navy typography.

### Section Headers
- **Format**: An inline flex row holding an orange mini-box (`■`, 8px × 8px) followed by an uppercase eyebrow in Royal Blue (`#0588CF`, tracking +0.08em). Below it rests the primary headline in Deep Navy (`#12304A`).

### Unit & Pricing Cards
- **Structure**: High-contrast cards showcasing facility photography at the top, unit volume/area pills (e.g., "1.5 m² / 4.5 m³"), clear inclusion bullets (climate control, 24/7 CCTV, pin code access), bold pricing typography (`price-lg`), and an immediate "Book Storage" orange CTA.
- **Featured Tag**: A vibrant orange corner badge or top banner reading "Most Popular" or "Best Value".

### Form Inputs & Size Estimator Controls
- **Inputs**: 48px height, white background, `#D5E2EC` border, Deep Navy input text, Slate placeholder. Focus rings glow with `#0588CF` (2px).
- **Steppers & Sliders**: Blue active rails with orange slider thumbs for visual estimation of boxes, furniture, and square meter requirements.

### Sticky Bottom Multi-Action Bar (Mobile & Tablet)
- **Structure**: A fixed 4-column container anchored to the bottom edge.
- **Segments**:
  1. *View Pricing*: Icon + slate navy label.
  2. *Book Storage*: High-contrast solid orange segment with bold white text.
  3. *Chat Support*: WhatsApp / Zalo / Messenger icon + navy label.
  4. *Call / Contact*: Phone icon + navy label.
